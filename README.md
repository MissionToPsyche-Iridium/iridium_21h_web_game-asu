**Project Executive Summary** 
This project is based on a mission to engage the public in space exploration, the metal-rich asteroid PsyChe, located between Mars and Jupiter. In fact, this spacecraft, which was launched by NASA in October 2023, is scheduled to arrive at Psyche in mid-2029 and study the asteroid from orbit. This mission will help us understand the composition of the planet’s core and gain insight into the early history of our solar system. 

As a NASA project to engage people of all ages and make space exploration more accessible, our project aims to create a web-based game that provides both education and entertainment. The game immerses players in the Psyche mission, taking them on a journey from the initial conception to the final stages of the mission. During the game, players solve interactive challenges or quizzes to deepen their understanding of the universe through the mission. 


Our game aims to provide accurate and easy-to-understand information about the Psyche mission and to inspire curiosity and excitement about the universe. The game features a simple 2D interface with engaging minigames, animations, and quizzes that help players learn about the fascinating world of asteroid exploration in an enjoyable way.
